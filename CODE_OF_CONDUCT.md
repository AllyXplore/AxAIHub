# Code of Conduct / 行为准则

## Short version / 简版

Be decent. Discuss the software, not the people. Assume the other side is trying to help.
保持体面。讨论软件本身，不针对个人。默认对方是出于善意。

## Scope / 适用范围

This applies to issues, pull requests, discussions and the community channel of this project — anywhere
people interact about AxAIHub.
适用于本项目的 issue、合并请求、讨论区与社区频道——任何围绕 AxAIHub 交流的场合。

## Expected / 我们期待

- Stay on topic and give concrete details. 切题，并提供具体信息。
- Accept that a request may be declined, and say why you disagree without escalating.
  接受需求可能被拒绝；有不同意见时说明理由，不升级冲突。
- Respect that this project is maintained in spare time. 理解本项目在业余时间维护。
- Use either English or Chinese; both are fine here. 中文或英文均可。

## Not acceptable / 不可接受

- Personal attacks, harassment, or discriminatory remarks of any kind.
  人身攻击、骚扰，或任何形式的歧视性言论。
- Publishing someone's private information. 公开他人的私人信息。
- Sexualised content, or any content involving minors in a sexual context.
  性化内容，或任何涉及未成年人的性相关内容。
- Spam, off-topic promotion, or repeatedly reopening settled matters.
  垃圾信息、无关推广，或反复重开已有定论的争议。
- Sharing cracked, repackaged or unofficially signed builds, or links to them.
  分享破解版、重打包版、非官方签名版本或其链接。

## Consequences / 处理方式

Depending on severity, the maintainer may edit or remove content, close a thread, or block an account
from participating. Decisions rest with the maintainer.
视严重程度，维护者可能编辑或删除内容、关闭讨论，或禁止某账号继续参与。最终决定由维护者作出。
